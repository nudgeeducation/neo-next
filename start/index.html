Getting started with NEO

This is yours.

A few short parts to help us understand how things are for you, what you enjoy and what you'd like to work on. It takes about ten minutes.

How it works

  • Nothing you put in here is saved or sent anywhere. It stays on this screen, and it's gone when you close this page.
  • You can skip anything, go back, or stop.
  • At the end you'll see your answers together. You choose which parts to show us, and you can keep a copy for yourself.
  • We ask so we can look after you properly and plan lessons that suit you. If something you show us makes us worried about your safety, we'd need to talk to someone who can help. We would always tell you first.

The person you're talking to can't see this screen unless you share it.

Part 1 of 4 · The Cornerstones

Where am I now?

The six Cornerstones are the things everyone needs to feel OK and ready to learn. Give each one a number: 1 means not great at the moment, 5 means going really well. There are no right answers.

Part 2 of 4 · The last two weeks

How have you been feeling?

These five come from the World Health Organization, so the words are exactly theirs. Please choose the answer closest to how you have been feeling over the last two weeks.

If any of this feels hard right now, you can tell the person you're talking to, or someone at home. You can also talk to Childline any time for free on 0800 1111, or text SHOUT to 85258.
